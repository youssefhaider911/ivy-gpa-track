How to install

The extension is free to download. Follow these steps in Google Chrome.

1

Download

Click the download button and save the ZIP file.

2

Extract the ZIP

Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.

3

Open Chrome Extensions

Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

4

Load the extension

Click Load unpacked and select the extracted IVY GPA Track folder.

5

Open IVY GPA Track

Open it from Chrome's Extensions menu and pin it if you want.

6

Enter your information

Enter your student name, grade, and school ID. These are information fields, not a school-account login.

Important: This free version is not in the Chrome Web Store, so Chrome requires Developer mode and Load unpacked.
